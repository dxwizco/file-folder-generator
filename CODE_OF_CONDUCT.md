# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

File & Folder Generator is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with the project.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives and encourage collaboration based on respect and professionalism.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Give and receive feedback respectfully.
- Consider different opinions and approaches.
- Focus discussions on the project and its improvement.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for everyone.

Examples of positive participation include:

- Suggesting improvements.
- Sharing ideas and technical perspectives.
- Reporting bugs with useful details.
- Helping other users.
- Reviewing contributions constructively.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language or behavior.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or personal information without permission.
- Abusive, insulting, or disrespectful communication.
- Any other behavior that reasonably discourages or prevents others from participating.

---

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreement is a normal and valuable part of open-source development.

When discussing technical decisions:

- Focus on the idea or implementation, not the person.
- Explain the reasoning behind your suggestions.
- Consider alternative approaches.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Assume good intentions while remaining respectful.

Different technical opinions are welcome. Constructive disagreement helps improve the project.

---

## Reporting Unacceptable Behavior

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior in an official File & Folder Generator community space, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

When possible, include:

- A description of the situation.
- Relevant links, messages, or examples.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the process described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than publicly reporting the issue.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers will make reasonable efforts to:

- Maintain a respectful and constructive project environment.
- Review contributions fairly and objectively.
- Address reported conduct issues appropriately.
- Help contributors understand and follow project expectations.

Maintainers may take appropriate action when unacceptable behavior occurs, including removing content or restricting participation in project spaces when necessary.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official File & Folder Generator project spaces and activities, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Code reviews
- Other official project communication spaces

It may also apply to conduct outside these spaces when it directly affects the safety or participation of members of the project community.

---

## Thank You

Thank you for helping make File & Folder Generator a respectful, constructive, and welcoming open-source project.
